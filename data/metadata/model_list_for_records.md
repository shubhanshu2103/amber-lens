## Same model, different plates (demo material)

- `M2BAX160MLC2` (ABB, both heldout): hf_073 reads IE4, 415 D V, 31.10 A, prod code V4012 161430-ADN; hf_077 reads IE3, 400 D V, 34.20 A, prod code H6343 161430-ADD. Same type, different ratings.
- `EPT0754` (tuning): hf_038/hf_048 are one Motors & Drives International plate; hf_247 is a Galt plate with the same model and ratings. Brand differs, ratings match.
- `1LA02864SE41` (Siemens): hf_286 is a US plate, 460 V 34.9 A 30 HP 60 Hz. The Indian 415 V 52 A 30 kW 50 Hz plate with the same ORD.NO. exists only as graphics (05, 026, 190), so it was rejected. The kit's 05 vs 037 conflict is an OCR artefact: 037 is an infographic and its 54.6/31.5 A is not printed on it.
- Datasheet disagreements: `M3538` (packet 1.6/.8 A, photo 2/1 A), `EM3615T` (packet 230/460 V 13.4/6.7 A, photo 208-230/460 V 13.9-13.4/6.7 A, older spec), `M2BAX180MLA4` (sheet is the 460 V 60 Hz -BDC variant, photo is the 415 V -BDCIN variant).
